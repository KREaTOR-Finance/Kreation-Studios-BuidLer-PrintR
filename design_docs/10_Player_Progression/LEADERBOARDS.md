# Leaderboards

Multi-dimensional to reward skill, not just grind.

## Session
- Per asset-session final score

## Timeboxed (Daily/Weekly/Monthly)
- WeightedScore = TotalPoints * sqrt(UniqueSessionsPlayed)

## Skill
- Win rate (min closes)
- Avg points per marker (min markers)
- Break rate (lower better)
- Forfeit rate (lower better)
- Leverage efficiency (points per risk unit)

## Creator
- Joins
- Retention score
- Quality score (joins + low forfeits + balanced volatility)

## UX rules
- show player row + ±2 neighbors
- inline badges (max 3)
- pinned trophy icon
- pagination
