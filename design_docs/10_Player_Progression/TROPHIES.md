# Trophies (Prestige)

Scarce awards; players can pin 3.

## Performance
- Session King: top 1% for a session
- Market Whisperer: perfect session (all closes positive, no breaks, no forfeits)
- Biggest Swing: largest single realized gain
- Surgeon: highest points per marker (min threshold)

## Seasonal
- Weekly Champion: #1 weekly
- Monthly Top 100
- Genesis Founder (launch season participation)

## Creator
- Crowd Favorite (weekly joins)
- Retention Builder (weekly)
- Quality Volatility (weekly)
