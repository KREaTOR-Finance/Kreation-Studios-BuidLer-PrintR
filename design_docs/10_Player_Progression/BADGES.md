# Badges (v1)

Badges are permanent achievements (no gameplay advantage). Start with ~20 badges.

## Skill
- Clean Closer: 10 closes without Break Point
- Trend Rider: 5 profitable closes in a row
- Contrarian: Win a SHORT when demand is strongly LONG (>=0.7)
- Precision Entry: Close within top 5% of peak favorable move
- Sniper: 3 closes within 1 tick of local peak/trough

## Risk & Control
- Edge Walker: Profit with Stability Meter under 10%
- Calm Hands: Win without leverage in high-volatility archetype
- Controlled Burn: Use leverage and never break across 3 sessions
- Diamond Nerves: Hold >= 12 ticks and close positive

## Consistency
- Daily Printer: play 5 days in a row
- Session Grinder: finish 20 sessions
- Never Left Open: 10 sessions, 0 forfeits
- Comeback: finish positive after being negative mid-session

## Parallel Play
- Juggler: manage 3 sessions at once
- Split Focus: two winning closes within 60s across sessions
- Quad Minded: touch all 4 sessions in one round
- Grid Master: 5 closes from GRID view

## Creator
- First Drop: create your first asset session
- Crowd Magnet: 25 joins on your created asset
