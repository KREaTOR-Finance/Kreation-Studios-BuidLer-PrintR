# Pricing & Bundles (Locked)

- **1 USDC = 4 Sessions**
- Session = one 20-minute asset session join (spectating is free)
- Credits are **non-transferable**, **non-redeemable**, and **for gameplay only**

## Bundles
- **$10 minimum** → **40 Sessions**
- **$100 bundle** → **500 Sessions**

## Consumption Rules
- 1 Session credit is consumed when player successfully joins a LIVE or FINAL_COMMIT_WARNING session.
- Joining during CLOSING is **spectator** and consumes **0 credits**.
- If session join fails before receiving a join-ack, credit is refunded (idempotent).
